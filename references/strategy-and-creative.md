# Planning, intent, and creative

Start with the business goal and evidence for the real offer. Group campaigns
and ad groups by distinct purchase intent. Context Hints describe a reasonable
need situation and are not precise targeting or a promise of a particular
conversation placement.

Each planned ad should identify its theme, headline, description, landing URL,
creative needs, claim references, assumptions, and review status. Keep claims
aligned with evidence and landing-page terms. Do not invent endorsements,
effects, scarcity, prices, inventory, or guarantees.

Experiments should define the primary metric, data-quality condition, maximum
loss, evaluation time, and stopping rule. Planning an experiment does not run
it.
