# Lite beginner workflow

Start with the offer, customer, goal, market, landing URL, total spend limit,
and measurable success action. Missing facts stay as questions or unknowns.

Lite produces local onboarding, campaign planning, budget scenarios, creative
briefs, landing checks, and measurement planning. It never inspects live tools,
accounts, schemas, or write capability.

Every budget result is an assumption, not an approval. Context Hints describe
need situations; they do not target private conversations or guarantee
delivery. Conversion conclusions require supplied evidence and verified
tracking; otherwise report click-level signals only.
